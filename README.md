# Introduction

PeerTrain is an AI-assisted training tool designed to help peer supporters develop their skills in supporting others through simulated practice, constructive feedback, and reflection.

### How PeerTrain works

**Practise**

Engage in realistic conversations with AI-simulated help-seekers, exploring challenging situations in peer support.

**Get feedback**

Receive feedback on your responses, review key moments, and consider alternative ways of responding.

**Reflect**

Reflect on your considerations, explore the feedback with an AI chatbot, or record your own thoughts.

### About this research

PeerTrain is a research prototype exploring how AI can help peer supporters develop their skills and confidence to give effective and sustainable support to others.

It focuses particularly on navigating interpersonal boundaries and sensitive situations while respecting the values of peer support.

### What PeerTrain is — and isn't

PeerTrain is designed for training and reflection, not for providing real-world peer support, clinical advice, or crisis intervention.

Its AI-simulated conversations and AI-generated feedback may be imperfect. They are intended to encourage learning and critical reflection, not to provide definitive answers about the “right” way to support someone.

PeerTrain is not a substitute for human-led training or supervision.
